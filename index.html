The Open Shelf

a small digital archive of books anyone can read, free

Books grow on this tree in alphabetical order. Tap a cover to read the book for free.

    Why we are building this archive

    Most people who want to read a classic never get past the first obstacle: finding a legitimate copy they can open without paying, signing up or guessing which site is safe.

    This archive gathers books that are free to read in one place. Each entry has its author, year and a short description, and its cover leads straight to a free, legal edition hosted by a trusted library.

    We are building it so that reading is not a privilege of whoever has a library card, a fast connection to a store, or a budget for books. It is also a study aid: students and researchers can browse by title, see when a work appeared, and open it in one tap.

    The archive is a living shelf. New books are added over time, and every entry points to the original free source rather than hosting copies itself.
